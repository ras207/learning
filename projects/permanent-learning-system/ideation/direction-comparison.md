## 3. What the comparison shows

**Evidence-backed versus assumption-driven.** The only first-hand evidence is the user's own testimony (E-001, E-002): current learning is not interactive, current or tailored, and six months of AI learning were unstructured. Every rating above is inference from the problem model and general learning-science knowledge. The three largest assumptions are A-008 (record-plus-session tutoring works), A-009 (people are available) and the untested state of existing products (WI-014).

**Dominant trade-offs.**

1. **Structure versus integration.** A is best at practice, sequencing and maintenance but integrates only by design. B integrates natively and builds artefacts, but leaves gaps and lets unused skills fade. They are strongest on each other's weaknesses.
2. **AI evidence versus human evidence.** A, B, C and E rely mostly on AI or self-assessment of capability, which is weakest exactly where B1 and the 3- and 10-year targets bite: "validate", "robust", "respected". D is strongest here but cannot carry the whole load (fit with hours, AI half, availability).
3. **Meta-work versus ownership.** C has the least meta-work but gives up the owned, cross-domain learner model (AM-3, C3) and the learning-while-building that D-002 wants. A and B cost a light build, most of which is itself one-year target learning.
4. **Privacy versus capability and cost (WI-013).** Only P2 fits C2 and the core of C3. It leaves session contents visible to a cloud provider. P3 fully meets C3 but likely breaks C2 and weakens tutoring **[general knowledge, not researched]**.

**Disconfirming conditions.**

- If A-008 fails, the curriculum-manager role in A, B and E weakens, and C or D gain relatively.
- If an existing product offers an exportable, cross-domain, user-held learner model (WI-014), C's weaknesses on criteria 1, 10 and 11 shrink and it may dominate on meta-work.
- If the user has ready access to credible mentors or peers (A-009), D's role should grow beyond calibration.
- If weekends cannot reliably hold project blocks (A-005), B weakens.

## 4. Recommendation

**Recommended broad direction: B's project spine run on A's owned record, with D's human judgement as periodic calibration, at position P2 and a light build.**

In plain terms: the user keeps their own record of targets, mastery and evidence. The AI uses it to choose the next step (D-001). Most learning happens through a continuing line of real, private projects at the economics-AI-policy intersection (B), with short AI-designed practice and refresh sessions in commute slots filling the gaps those projects expose and keeping unused skills alive (A). From time to time the user tests their work and reasoning against credible people (D), which is also how "validate", "robust" and "respected" get evidence the AI cannot supply.

**Why.** Each part covers another's weakest criteria: B supplies integration, artefacts and motivation; A supplies sequencing, maintenance, commute fit and the owned record; D supplies external evidence and the strongest guard for B1. P2 is the only privacy position within budget. The light build is mostly one-year target learning (D-002), which keeps M10 manageable.

**Why the credible alternatives are weaker as the main direction.**

- **A alone** gives the best structure but no native integration, AI-only evidence, and exercises-first learning that repeats the "content not capability" risk the user set out to escape (B2).
- **B alone** lets foundations and unused capabilities decay and has no maintenance mechanism, which fails a *permanent* system (B3, AM-10).
- **C** is the lowest-effort improvement but gives up the owned learner model and cross-domain integration, the two things no current approach provides (problem-model section 6). It is the strongest fallback if WI-014 research reveals a suitable product or if meta-work proves unmanageable.
- **D as the core** depends on other people's time and availability (A-009) and does little for the AI-systems half.
- **E** is a sound *use of the day job* inside the recommended direction, but deferring integration by design works against the three-year target.

**Honest cost of the recommendation.** It is a hybrid and therefore more complex than any single direction. The biggest risk is that three mechanisms together create the meta-work WI-006 warns about. It also rests on A-008, which is untested. These go to the pre-Gate-5 focused challenge.
