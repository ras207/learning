# Permanent Learning System

Project ID: `permanent-learning-system`
Project slug: `permanent-learning-system`

## Initial idea

I want to create a permanent personal learning system that helps me continuously develop genuinely useful capabilities over years, rather than simply completing courses or consuming content. The system should understand what I ultimately want to become capable of doing, model those capabilities and my evolving level of mastery, and use evidence from learning sessions, projects, assessments and real-world performance to decide what I should work on next. AI should act as an adaptive tutor, curriculum manager and learning orchestrator—designing sessions, challenging me, identifying weaknesses, connecting different areas of knowledge and adapting the programme as I develop—while ensuring that I retain the reasoning, understanding and judgement rather than outsourcing them to AI. Learning should increasingly happen through real projects and consequential activity, producing a persistent body of knowledge, evidence, software, research, creative work and other artefacts that becomes valuable in its own right. Ultimately, I want a lifelong system that gets progressively better at understanding me, choosing high-value learning opportunities and helping me turn broad ambitions across economics, technology, AI, business and other interests into demonstrable real-world capability.
